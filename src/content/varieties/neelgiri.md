Neelgiri Grass develops a dense, carpet-like structure that gives the lawn a rich and well-covered appearance. Its dark-green colour and softer feel make it an attractive choice for lawns and landscaped spaces.

It takes approximately 3–4 months to establish and generally requires 3–4 cuttings in a year. Its dense growth helps create a lush, uniform lawn with proper care.
