Neelgiri is our go-to recommendation whenever the area is large or the lawn
needs to take heavy use — parks, open grounds, commercial plots. It spreads
quickly, fills in bare patches on its own, and tolerates Haryana's summer
heat well, which keeps upkeep costs down for bigger projects.
