The best time to lay grass depends on your local weather and the grass type you are planting. In Haryana, the most comfortable periods are generally early monsoon conditions and the cooler months when the soil stays moist and the heat is less extreme.

Laying grass during the very hottest week of summer can be risky because the soil heats up quickly and the young roots may struggle to establish. If you do plant in summer, extra watering and careful monitoring become essential.

For homeowners planning a fresh lawn, spring and early monsoon often provide the strongest start. This gives the grass enough time to root before temperatures rise again. If you are using a more heat-tolerant variety, autumn or early winter may also be useful for establishing a stronger lawn.

The key is to avoid extremes. A stable soil temperature and regular moisture usually lead to a far stronger lawn than forcing grass to establish during harsh heat.
