Neelgiri grass is often the smartest option for big open spaces. It handles heat well and grows quickly enough to cover large areas without constant hand-holding.

For large lawns, schools, parks, and wide residential plots, this grass offers a strong balance of coverage and durability. It is less fussy than more delicate lawn varieties and often spreads naturally, which helps it close gaps faster.

This makes it especially useful where the area is visible from a distance and the goal is a full, green look without excessive maintenance. In Haryana's dry and hot conditions, it often performs better than lawns that demand more regular attention.

A few reasons people choose Neelgiri grass:

- It is strong in high heat.
- It spreads well across open ground.
- It is practical for larger spaces that need quick coverage.
- It is easier to maintain if you are not checking the lawn daily.

If you have a large garden or an open front area and want a dependable lawn without intensive upkeep, Neelgiri is usually a strong fit.
