Many homeowners in Ambala struggle with the same question: should they choose Korean grass or Selection No. 1? The answer depends on how the lawn is used and how much maintenance you want to provide.

Selection No. 1 is a dependable everyday choice. It handles heat well, fills in steadily, and gives a neat finish without needing constant attention. It is often chosen for family lawns and medium-sized outdoor spaces where durability matters.

Korean grass has a softer and more refined appearance. It looks especially attractive in front lawns and decorative spaces, where a fine textured, manicured finish is the goal. The tradeoff is that it usually needs more regular watering and upkeep during peak summer.

If your priority is a low-stress lawn that still looks good, Selection No. 1 is the safer option. If your priority is a premium visual finish and you can maintain it carefully, Korean grass can look excellent.

In short:

- Choose Selection No. 1 for easy upkeep and heat tolerance.
- Choose Korean grass for a polished, showroom-style lawn.
