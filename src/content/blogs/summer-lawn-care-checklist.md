Summer can put a lot of stress on a lawn, especially in Haryana where daytime heat can be intense. The best way to keep grass healthy is to follow a simple routine instead of reacting only when the lawn begins showing damage.

Start with checking soil moisture rather than guessing. If the ground is dry several inches below the surface, the lawn needs deeper watering. Also, keep mowing at a moderate height and avoid cutting too short, because shorter blades lose moisture faster in strong heat.

Use early morning watering whenever possible, and keep an eye on any areas that stay dry or start to discolor. If one section of the lawn struggles more than others, that may mean the soil or sunlight is uneven there.

A well-kept summer lawn is usually the result of light but consistent attention. It is easier to prevent stress than repair it after the grass has already weakened.
