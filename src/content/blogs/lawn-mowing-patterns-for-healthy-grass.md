Mowing is more than just cutting grass short. The pattern you use affects how evenly your lawn grows and how strong it stays through the season.

A common mistake is cutting the grass in the same direction every single time. Over time, this can create stress points and make the lawn look uneven. Rotating your mowing pattern helps keep the grass blades upright and supports a more even growth pattern.

For most lawns, mow when the grass is dry and keep the blade height steady. Cutting too short can weaken the grass roots, especially in hot weather. A slightly taller lawn also holds moisture better and shades the soil, which helps during Haryana summers.

Aim for a moderate cut and use a clean blade. If the lawn is heavy or the grass is growing quickly, avoid cutting more than one-third of the height at once.
