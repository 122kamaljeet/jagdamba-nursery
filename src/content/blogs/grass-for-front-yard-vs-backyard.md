Front yards and backyards often have different needs. The front yard is usually about the first impression, while the backyard may be used more for families, play, and daily activity.

That means the grass type should match the purpose. A decorative front lawn often benefits from a fine, neatly kept look, while a backyard may need something more resilient, durable, and easier to maintain under regular use.

For front lawns, many people prefer a cleaner and more refined appearance. For backyard lawns, the priority is often resilience and easy upkeep. If the area receives heavy foot traffic or family activity, a tougher option may be the better choice.

This is why we often ask about the use of the space before recommending a grass type. It is not only about appearance; it is about which lawn will look good and stay healthy under real conditions.
