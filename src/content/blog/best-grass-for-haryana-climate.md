Haryana's summers are long and hot, and not every lawn grass holds up to
that without heavy watering. Here's how our three varieties compare for
local conditions.

**Selection No. 1** is the safest all-round choice for a home lawn — it
tolerates heat well and only needs mowing every two to three weeks.

**Korean grass** gives the most manicured look but wants more consistent
watering through peak summer, so it suits gardens where someone is
maintaining it regularly.

**Neelgiri** is the most heat-tolerant of the three and spreads on its own,
which makes it the practical pick for larger open areas where you don't
want to babysit every patch.

If you're unsure, tell us your lawn size and how much time you can put into
upkeep, and we'll recommend one.
